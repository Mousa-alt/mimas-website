From Bayonne to the World

Born in the historic culinary heart of Bayonne, France, our journey began with the scent of maturing rind and the crisp bite of Alpine mornings. Trained in the most prestigious European ateliers, our founder honed the ancient craft of affineur, learning that cheese is not just food—it is a living, breathing testament to terroir.

Recognized as a Vice-Champion du Monde des Fromagers, this expertise has been transplanted to the vibrant tables of Dubai, merging traditional French soul with global curiosity.
