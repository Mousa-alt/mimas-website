How to Enjoy Your Board

1.

Take your cheeseboard out of the fridge 30 to 45 minutes before serving. Cheese tastes best at room temperature, when its full flavour comes alive.

2.

Your board is thoughtfully arranged using the art of pairing. Each cheese is placed next to its perfect condiment.

3.

Start by tasting the cheese on its own, then try it with the pairing beside it.

4.

For the best experience, enjoy the cheeses from mild to bold. Save the blue cheese for last.

Allergy & Ingredient Information

info

Our cheeseboards contain dairy products.

info

Some boards include nuts such as almonds and walnuts. While we take care in preparation, all boards are assembled in the same area and we cannot guarantee the absence of cross-contamination.

info

We use high-quality cheeses, some made from raw milk. These are not recommended for pregnant women.

info

Our dried fruits are natural and free from sulfites.
